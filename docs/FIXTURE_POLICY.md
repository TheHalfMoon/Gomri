# Gomrey Fixture and Test-Data Policy

**Status:** P02-G4 baseline.
**Machine-readable contract:** `config/fixture-policy.json`.
**Enforcement:** `scripts/check-fixtures.mjs` (deterministic, no network).

## Allowed fixtures

- Synthetic fixtures generated by a checked-in script with a recorded seed.
- Public benchmark inputs that are explicitly redistributable.
- Explicitly licensed data with attribution preserved.

## Prohibited fixtures

- Real user secrets, API keys, or tokens.
- Private health or business data.
- Donor credentials or production dumps.
- Unlicensed scraped personal data.

## Provenance and regeneration

Each fixture directory documents its source, license, creation date, and
generator or acquisition method. Generated fixtures record the exact generator
script and seed so another engineer can reproduce them. Large corpora are
referenced, not committed; committed fixtures stay small and bounded.

## Verification

`scripts/check-fixtures.mjs` scans the configured test roots for denied
patterns (credential-like material, donor credential filenames, production dump
filenames) and fails visibly on any match. `scripts/scan-secrets.mjs` provides
the complementary repository-wide credential guard.
