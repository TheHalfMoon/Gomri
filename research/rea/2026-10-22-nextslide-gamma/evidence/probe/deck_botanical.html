Five commitments for 2026

  • River interception at 1,400 high-load catchments worldwide
  • Producer-funded reuse infrastructure in 12 coastal markets
  • Single-use polymer phase-out in packaging and hospitality
  • Open chemical-recycling feedstock contracts for PET and nylon
  • Verified marine habitat restoration across 200,000 hectares

Where we are vs. where we need to be

Today

Roughly 9% of ocean plastic is removed each year, concentrated in a handful of high-visibility cleanup projects that rarely touch the highest-volume sources.

2026 target

A 73% reduction in annual ocean-bound plastic by 2030, achieved through upstream interception rather than post-hoc recovery.

Speaker note for slide 3 (two-column). Expand with regional detail and cite the 2026 audit.

Prevention now, not cleanup later

Cleanup first

Recovery happens after damage is done. Cost per tonne stays above $4,000 and ecological benefit is local and short-lived.

Prevention first

Intercepting at source prevents emission entirely. Cost per tonne falls below $180 and avoided emissions compound every year.
